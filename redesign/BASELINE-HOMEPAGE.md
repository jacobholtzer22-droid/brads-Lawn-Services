# "Before" — homepage structure at the start of the visual redesign

On record for the client report. Content and facts here are correct; the
*presentation* is what this pass replaces.

1. **Header** — sticky white bar, logo wordmark left, text nav right, phone link
   + green "Request A Quote" button. Services is a hover dropdown.
2. **Hero** — full-bleed photo (striped lawn) at 40% opacity over near-black,
   green pill "A Family Business Since 2010", H1 "Lawn care in Battle Creek done
   with an edge", paragraph, two stacked CTAs, three inline meta items.
3. **Trust strip** — light gray band, four centered stats (years / 24-7 / Family
   / core services) over a divider, then a row of HomeAdvisor + Thumbtack badges
   linked to the live profile.
4. **Services grid** — three-up cards, each a 16:10 photo above icon + title +
   one-line description + "Learn more". Flat borders, no hover treatment beyond
   a border-colour change.
5. **"Service With An Edge"** — dark slate band, two columns: motto + origin
   paragraph + button on the left, three bordered pillar cards stacked right.
6. **Reviews** — heading + "Read all reviews" link, then a full-bleed horizontal
   scroll-snap carousel of six review cards with amber stars.
7. **Service area** — light band, two columns: copy + four ZIP chips + button on
   the left, single 4:3 photo right.
8. **FAQ** — centered single column, six `<details>` accordions on hairline
   dividers with a plus-icon affordance.
9. **Closing CTA** — solid green band, centered heading, phone button + outlined
   quote button.
10. **Footer** — dark slate, four columns (services / company / service area /
    contact), then a thin bottom bar with copyright and motto.

**Honest read:** the section *order* is sound and conversion-oriented, but the
execution is generic — flat cards, uniform section rhythm, little depth or
motion, type that never asserts itself, and photography used as small contained
rectangles rather than as the main event. It reads as a competent template, not
as a designed brand.
